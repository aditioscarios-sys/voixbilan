VoixBilan

AI-Powered Voice Bookkeeping for African Micro-Enterprises

VoixBilan is an AI-powered financial record-keeping and monitoring platform designed to help African micro-entrepreneurs record business transactions through WhatsApp voice messages.

The platform also aims to help NGOs, cooperatives, and development programmes monitor the businesses they support through a web dashboard with structured financial information and monitoring indicators.

Our goal is to make business record-keeping more accessible, practical, and inclusive.

The Problem

Many micro-entrepreneurs struggle to maintain consistent records of their sales, expenses, stock purchases, credit transactions, and repayments.

At the same time, NGOs, cooperatives, and development programmes often need reliable, structured information to monitor the progress of the businesses they support.

Traditional bookkeeping methods can be time-consuming and difficult to maintain, especially for entrepreneurs who face barriers to written record-keeping and digital financial tools.

Our Solution

VoixBilan offers a conversational approach to business record-keeping.

Entrepreneurs can report transactions through WhatsApp messages. AI processes the submitted information and extracts relevant financial details, which can then be stored and reviewed through the VoixBilan web dashboard.

VoixBilan also aims to improve accessibility through support for African local languages. Improving the accuracy and reliability of local-language interpretation remains an important area for development and field testing.

How It Works

Transaction input: A user reports a business transaction through a WhatsApp voice or text message.

AI processing: The Google Gemini API helps interpret the message and extract relevant transaction information.

Data storage: The extracted information is saved to the application's database.

Dashboard monitoring: Recorded transactions can be reviewed through the VoixBilan web dashboard.

Business insights: Financial summaries and monitoring indicators help users and supporting organisations review business activity.

Key Features

WhatsApp-based transaction reporting.

AI-assisted interpretation of voice and text messages.

Recording of sales, expenses, stock purchases, credit transactions, and repayments.

Web dashboard for reviewing business activity.

Business and group management.

Financial summaries, trends, and comparisons.

CSV data export and printable PDF reports.

Continued development toward more reliable support for African local languages.

Technology Stack

AI processing: Google Gemini API.

WhatsApp integration: Baileys-based WhatsApp Web integration.

Web application: VoixBilan web dashboard.

Database: Shared database connecting the WhatsApp integration and dashboard.

Additional framework, database engine, and infrastructure details can be added after verification against the codebase.

Current Status

VoixBilan has a functional prototype connecting WhatsApp-based transaction reporting with a web dashboard.

The current dashboard demonstration uses fictitious business data. External user testing has not yet been completed.

The next development priorities are:

Testing with micro-entrepreneurs and supporting organisations.

Improving transaction extraction and classification accuracy.

Improving voice recognition and interpretation, particularly for African local languages.

Evaluating and strengthening the reliability of transaction recording.

Preparing the platform for field testing and potential deployment.

Limitations and Responsible Use

AI-generated transaction records may contain errors, particularly when amounts, transaction types, or local-language expressions are ambiguous.

The prototype requires further testing to improve reliability. Uncertain transactions should be confirmed rather than automatically treated as verified financial records.

The current WhatsApp integration uses Baileys, an unofficial WhatsApp Web integration. A production deployment will require further technical and operational assessment, including evaluation of an official WhatsApp integration.

VoixBilan is a financial record-keeping and monitoring tool. Its estimates are not a substitute for certified accounting or professional financial advice.
